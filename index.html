How it works

Three steps. No software to install.

01

Share your patient list

Send us a simple export of past patients — name, phone, last visit, test type. We handle everything from there.

02

We call and remind

Our AI agent calls each due patient in their own language, reminds them politely, and offers to book a slot.

03

You get bookings

Confirmed slots arrive in your existing workflow. We send a WhatsApp confirmation to the patient. You collect as normal.

04

Pay only on results

We charge a small percentage of the revenue we actually recover. If no patients return, you owe nothing.
