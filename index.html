How it works

Three steps. Nothing to install.

Send us a patient export

Name, phone, last test and last visit date, from the lab software you already use. We work out who is due.

We call, in their language

Our voice assistant calls each due patient in Kannada, Hindi or English, says it's calling for your lab, and offers a slot.

You get a daily booking sheet

Bookings arrive as a simple daily sheet for each branch. Your team confirms them and collects as usual.

Proof, not guesswork

We only count patients who came back because of us.

Some patients would have returned anyway. So we set aside a small group from your list and don't call them. The difference between the two groups is what we actually brought back, and it's the only number we charge for.

Questions labs ask

Before you say yes.

Do we need to install anything or connect our lab software?

No. A spreadsheet export from your existing software is enough. Bookings come back as a daily sheet your team already knows how to use.

We already send SMS reminders. Is this still useful?

That's exactly what the uncalled group measures. Both groups still get your usual reminders, so you see only the extra patients our calls bring in. If there aren't any, you'll know.

Which languages do the calls use?

Kannada, Hindi and English today. The call uses the patient's preferred language when your records have it.

What patient data do you need, and what happens to it?

Only name, phone number, last test and last visit date. We use it only to call your patients for you, and we delete it when you ask. Details are in our privacy policy.

What if a patient gets annoyed?

They can say "don't call me" and they won't be called again. Retries are capped, and we review the recordings during the pilot to catch anything that sounds off.
