How it works

Four steps. No software to install.

01

Share your patient list

Send us a simple export of past patients — name, phone, last visit, test type. We handle everything from there.

02

We call and remind

Our AI agent calls each due patient in their own language, reminds them politely, and offers to book a slot.

03

You get bookings

Booking requests arrive as a simple daily sheet for your branch team to confirm. You collect as normal.

04

Pay only on results

We charge a flat fee per verified returning patient, measured against a holdout group. If no patients return, you owe nothing.
