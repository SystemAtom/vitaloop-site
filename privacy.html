Privacy

Vitaloop is operated by Novara Systems Private Limited.

Our role

The diagnostic lab you visit is the data fiduciary for your information. Novara Systems Private Limited (Vitaloop) acts only as a data processor, handling patient data on the lab's behalf and on its instructions.

Where data is hosted

Patient data is hosted in India, in the Mumbai region.

How data is used

Data shared by the lab is used only to send test reminders and book return visits. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Access

Only the Vitaloop team can access patient data; we never sell or share it.

Deletion

Data is deleted on request, or after the pilot ends.

Call recording

Calls may be recorded. Patients are told about recording at the start of the call.

Calls are made by an automated assistant, which says so at the start of every call.

Opting out

Patients can opt out of further calls at any time, on any call.
